01

Nonlinear exponential-affine bounds

Represents probability distributions using interval partitions equipped with exponential-affine density bounds, enabling sound and precise approximation of complex distributions over both bounded and unbounded supports.

02

Sound transformers

Abstract transformers for high-dimensional affine operation, nonlinear ReLU operation, non-trivial Bayesian inference operation..

03

From Bayesian Neural Networks(BNNs) to general probabilistic programs.

Enables sound uncertainty quantification in BNNs, capturing entire output distributions and neuron-level uncertainty propagation. Further extended to general probabilistic programs with Bayesian inference and bounded loops.
