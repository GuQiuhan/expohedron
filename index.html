01

Exponential-affine bounds

The support is split into pieces. On each piece the log-density is enclosed by a secant and a tangent, so the density is enclosed by exp(a·x + b): tangent above and secant below for log-concave families, the other way round for log-convex ones.

02

Sound transformers

Bayesian inference adds prior and likelihood bounds in log space and normalises by an interval Z ∈ [Z_L, Z_U]. ReLU splits off an atom at 0 with bounded mass. Scaling maps exp(a·x + b) to exp(a/c·x + b − log|c|).

03

Masses stay probabilities

When a bound is integrated to get a probability, the result is capped at 1, so even a non-integrable tail bound, such as a flat tail, stays sound. The normaliser Z is integrated without the cap.
